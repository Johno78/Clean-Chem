Cif Professional Oxy-Gel

Active oxygenlifts dirt fast

Cif Professional Oxy-Gel is an active-oxygen gel cleaner for daily use on floors and all washable surfaces — walls, tiles, protected and unprotected floors. The active oxygen forms micro-bubbles that lift dirt, which the gel then removes quickly. No rinsing needed when used diluted.

Before you start

  • For professional/trade use only.
  • Full safety information is in the product's Safety Data Sheet — ask us if you need a copy.

How to use it

Use neat on tough, localised stains — dilute for general daily cleaning of larger areas.

Small areas or tough stains — neat

  1. Pour Oxy-Gel onto a damp sponge or directly onto the surface.
  2. Clean the area and leave for 1–2 minutes.
  3. Rinse.

Large areas — diluted

  1. Dilute 40–80ml in 8L of water — the gel dissolves immediately.
  2. Apply the solution with a cloth or mop.
  3. No rinsing needed.

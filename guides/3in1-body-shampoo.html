3in1 Body Shampoo is a ready-to-use hair, body and face wash suited to leisure centres, gyms, hotels and other guest changing facilities. No dilution — dispense straight from the bottle or wall pump.

Before you start

  • Avoid contact with eyes — rinse thoroughly with water if it happens.
  • For external use only.

How to use it

Ready to use — no mixing needed.

Using it

  1. Dispense a small amount into the hand.
  2. Apply to wet hair, body or face and work into a lather.
  3. Rinse thoroughly with water.

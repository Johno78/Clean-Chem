Surf Professional White is a biological laundry powder with an extra strong whiteness formula and fragrance-boosting technology. The enzymes work efficiently even at low temperatures, giving strong stain removal without needing a hot wash.

Before you start

  • Not suitable for silk or wool.
  • Suitable for manually fed washing machines.
  • For professional/trade use only.
  • Full safety information is in the product's Safety Data Sheet — ask us if you need a copy.

How to use it

One 8.4kg box gives around 140 washes — roughly 60g per average load.

Machine wash

  1. Check garment care labels and choose the appropriate wash cycle and temperature.
  2. Add the powder to the dispenser drawer — around 60g for an average load.
  3. Don't overload the washing machine.
  4. Run the wash as normal.

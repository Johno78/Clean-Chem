Pine Disinfectant is a general-purpose disinfectant with a long-lasting pine fragrance, suitable for drains, floors, sinks and toilets. Dilute for everyday mopping and wiping, or use neat on small areas needing stronger disinfection.

Before you start

  • Wear gloves for prolonged contact.
  • Not suitable for non-washable surfaces (untreated or oiled wood, carpets, soft furnishings, polished furniture, wallpaper, painted surfaces, aluminium).
  • Test in an inconspicuous area first.
  • Full safety information is in the product's Safety Data Sheet — ask us if you need a copy.

How to use it

Use diluted for everyday cleaning, or neat for stronger disinfection of small areas.

General cleaning — diluted

  1. Dilute 1 part disinfectant to 30 parts water — a stronger mix can be made for heavier soiling.
  2. Apply with a mop or cloth to floors, sinks, drains and other washable hard surfaces.

Disinfecting small areas — neat

  1. Apply neat and leave for 5 minutes.
  2. Wipe with a wet cloth.

Flash Pro Spray Cleaner With Bleach cleans and disinfects hard surfaces in one step. Ready to use straight from the trigger bottle — no dilution needed.

Before you start

  • Never mix with other chemicals, especially acidic products — this releases dangerous chlorine gas.
  • Ensure good ventilation when using.
  • Full safety information is in the product's Safety Data Sheet — ask us if you need a copy.

How to use it

Ready to use straight from the trigger bottle — no mixing needed.

Cleaning & disinfecting

  1. Spray directly onto the surface.
  2. Wipe with a clean cloth.
  3. Leave to air dry for full disinfection contact time.

Suma Multipurpose D2.3 is a super-concentrated, 4-in-1 cleaner that replaces four separate products — floor cleaner, glass cleaner, general-purpose surface cleaner and hand dishwashing detergent — with one SmartDose bottle. It needs no water connection, so it's fully portable — ideal for smaller kitchens or where space is tight. The dispenser has two dose settings, giving the right strength whether you're filling a spray bottle or a bucket/sink.

Before you start

  • Wear gloves and eye protection when handling the concentrate or fitting a new SmartDose bottle.
  • For professional/trade use only.
  • Store away from extremes of temperature.
  • Full safety information is in the product's Safety Data Sheet — ask us if you need a copy.

How to use it

One bottle, two dose settings — pick the icon that matches what you're filling.

Spray bottle — glass & general surfaces

  1. Fill a 750ml spray bottle with water.
  2. Turn the dispenser head to the low (bottle) dose setting and press to dose the concentrate in.
  3. Shake gently before use.
  4. Spray onto the surface and wipe clean with a cloth.
  5. Rinse food-contact surfaces with clean water and leave to air dry.

Bucket or sink — floors & hand dishwashing

  1. Fill a bucket or sink with water.
  2. Turn the dispenser head to the high (bucket/sink) dose setting and press to dose the concentrate in.
  3. For floors: apply with a mop and let dry.
  4. For dishwashing: wash items, rinse with clean water, and let dry.

Suma Star-Plus D1 is a highly concentrated hand dishwashing liquid for pots, pans, crockery, glass and all washable utensils. It's a neutral-pH formula with a stable lather, so it's gentle on hands while still cutting through grease and dried-on food, and it rinses off streak-free. It's dosed through a Divermite Plus dispenser fitted at the pot wash sink.

Before you start

  • Neutral pH — gentler than our other concentrates, but the neat pouch concentrate can still irritate eyes on contact, so avoid splashes when fitting a new pouch.
  • Only use with the Divermite Plus dispenser (not the standard Divermite) — it's designed specifically for this product's dosing.
  • For professional/trade use only.
  • Store the sealed pouch away from extremes of temperature.
  • Full safety information is in the product's Safety Data Sheet — ask us if you need a copy.

How to use it

One push of the Divermite Plus dispenser doses the sink automatically — no measuring needed.

Washing up at the sink

  1. Fill the sink with hand-hot water.
  2. Dispense 1 push (15ml) per 40L of water from the Divermite Plus dispenser as the sink fills.
  3. Wash items with a cloth, brush or scouring pad.
  4. Rinse thoroughly with clean water to remove any detergent residue.
  5. Allow items to air dry fully before storing.

For heavily soiled items, a second push gives a stronger solution — adjust to the level of soiling.

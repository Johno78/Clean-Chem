SURE Cleaner & Degreaser is a concentrated, plant-based alkaline degreaser for heavily greasy kitchen equipment and surfaces — cooker hoods, cooker tops, worktops and tables. It's also strong enough for deep floor cleaning and stripping light wax build-up. It's part of Diversey's SURE range: 100% biodegradable, no fragrances or dyes. We stock it in two formats — as a Divermite pouch, and as a SmartDose bottle — so check which dispenser you're using below.

Before you start

  • Alkaline in use (pH ~11) — wear gloves and eye protection when handling the concentrate.
  • Not suitable for aluminium, copper or brass — use SURE Interior & Surface Cleaner instead on soft metals.
  • For professional/trade use only.
  • Store the sealed pouch away from extremes of temperature.
  • Full safety information is in the product's Safety Data Sheet — ask us if you need a copy.

How to use it

Two dispensing formats, same degreaser — use whichever matches the equipment on your wall.

Divermite pouch — spray cleaning

  1. Fill a 750ml spray bottle with water and dose from the Divermite dispenser to give a 1.5% solution.
  2. Remove any loose debris first.
  3. Spray onto the surface and wipe clean with a damp cloth.
  4. Rinse food-contact surfaces with clean water and leave to air dry.

Divermite pouch — bucket & floor cleaning

  1. Fill a bucket with water and dose from the Divermite dispenser to give a 0.5% solution.
  2. For heavily soiled surfaces, apply the solution and leave it to work for a few minutes before scrubbing.
  3. Clean with a mop, scrubbing pad or floor machine.
  4. Rinse and leave to air dry.

SmartDose bottle

  1. Fill a 750ml spray bottle with water.
  2. Turn the yellow head to the spray-bottle setting, pull it up fully, then press down to dose the concentrate into the bottle.
  3. Shake the bottle gently before use.
  4. Remove any loose debris, spray onto the surface and wipe with a cloth.
  5. Rinse food-contact surfaces with clean water and leave to air dry.

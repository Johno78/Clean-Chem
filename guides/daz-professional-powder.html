Daz Professional Powder is a general-purpose laundry detergent for everyday washing. Dosed into the machine's dispenser drawer — the exact amount depends on load size, soiling and water hardness.

Before you start

  • Wear gloves for prolonged contact or hand washing.
  • Avoid contact with eyes.
  • Full safety information is in the product's Safety Data Sheet — ask us if you need a copy.

How to use it

As a starting point for a normal load, medium soiling and medium-hard water:

Machine wash

  1. Check garment care labels and choose the appropriate wash cycle and temperature.
  2. Add 50–60g of powder to the dispenser drawer for a normal load.
  3. Increase the dose for heavier soiling or harder water.
  4. Run the wash as normal.

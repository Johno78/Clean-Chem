Fairy Washing Up Liquid is a concentrated detergent for manual dishwashing — cuts through grease with a small amount per sink. We stock the standard formula in 900ml and 5L, plus Fairy Platinum (820ml) for extra grease-cutting power, and Fairy Platinum Antibacterial (820ml) where hygiene is a priority.

Before you start

  • Avoid contact with eyes — rinse thoroughly with water if it happens.
  • Keep out of reach of children.

How to use it

Ready to use — just a small amount per sink of water.

Manual washing up

  1. Fill the sink or bowl with warm water.
  2. Add a small squeeze of washing up liquid and mix to create suds.
  3. Wash items, then rinse and air dry or towel dry.
  4. Top up with a little more liquid if the suds start to run thin.

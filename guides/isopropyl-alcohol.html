Isopropyl Alcohol 99.9% (IPA) is a high-purity solvent used for cleaning and disinfecting a wide range of surfaces, electronics and equipment. It evaporates quickly and leaves no residue, making it ideal where a fast-drying, streak-free finish matters. Used neat for general cleaning and degreasing, or diluted to around 70% for surface disinfection — 100% alcohol actually evaporates too quickly to give bacteria and viruses enough contact time to be killed effectively.

Before you start

  • Highly flammable. Keep away from heat, sparks, open flames and hot surfaces — no smoking nearby.
  • Use in a well-ventilated area.
  • Not suitable for all plastics — test on an inconspicuous area first.
  • Store in a tightly sealed container, in a cool, dry place away from ignition sources.
  • Full safety information is in the product's Safety Data Sheet — ask us if you need a copy.

How to use it

Use neat for degreasing and electronics; dilute for surface disinfection.

General cleaning & degreasing — neat

  1. Apply directly with a cloth or swab, or use a spray bottle.
  2. Wipe the surface. It will evaporate quickly, leaving no residue.

Surface disinfection — diluted

  1. Dilute to around 70% — roughly 7 parts IPA to 3 parts water.
  2. Apply to the surface with a spray bottle or cloth, ensuring it's thoroughly wetted.
  3. Allow to air dry.

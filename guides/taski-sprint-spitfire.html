TASKI Sprint Spitfire is a ready-to-use, heavy-duty cleaner for tough stains on alkaline-resistant hard surfaces — grease, ink, marker, lipstick, black heel marks, smoke films and light graffiti. No dilution — just spray and wipe.

Before you start

  • Not for water- or alkali-sensitive surfaces, painted surfaces, or food-contact surfaces.
  • Test material compatibility on a small, inconspicuous area first.
  • For professional/trade use only.
  • Full safety information is in the product's Safety Data Sheet — ask us if you need a copy.

How to use it

Ready to use straight from the trigger bottle — the method depends on how tough the stain is.

General stains

  1. Spray onto a cloth or sponge.
  2. Apply to the stained surface, agitating briefly if needed.
  3. Wipe clean.

Tough stains

  1. Spray directly onto the stained area.
  2. Scrub to speed up the cleaning process.
  3. Leave for 1 minute contact time.
  4. Wipe clean with a dry cloth.

No rinsing needed, except on stainless steel surfaces.

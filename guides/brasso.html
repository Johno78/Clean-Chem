Brasso Metal Polish

Brasso removes tarnish from brass, copper, chrome and stainless steel, restoring a bright shine. We stock it as a liquid or as pre-impregnated wadding — the liquid gives more control for larger or detailed items, while the wadding is quick and mess-free for smaller jobs. Both are mildly abrasive, so use sparingly and don't over-polish.

Before you start

  • Flammable liquid and vapour — keep away from heat, sparks and open flames.
  • Use in a well-ventilated area; avoid breathing in vapours or dust.
  • Wear gloves and eye protection.
  • Test on an inconspicuous area first.
  • Full safety information is in the product's Safety Data Sheet — ask us if you need a copy.

How to use it

Ready to use — no dilution for either format.

Liquid

  1. Apply a small amount to a soft cloth.
  2. Rub onto the metal surface using firm, circular motions.
  3. Leave to dry to a haze, then buff off with a clean, dry cloth to a shine.

Wadding

  1. Remove a piece of wadding from the tin.
  2. Polish the surface evenly, using firm pressure.
  3. Buff off with a clean, dry cloth to a shine.

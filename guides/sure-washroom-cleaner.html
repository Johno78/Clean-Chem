SURE Washroom Cleaner is a concentrated, plant-based alkaline cleaner for washrooms and other wet areas — effective on hard, water- and alkali-resistant surfaces. Regular use helps cut down limescale build-up. It's dosed through the SmartDose bottle system.

Before you start

  • Alkaline in use (pH ≈ 11) — wear gloves and eye protection when handling the concentrate.
  • For professional/trade use only.
  • Store away from extremes of temperature.
  • Full safety information is in the product's Safety Data Sheet — ask us if you need a copy.

How to use it

The SmartDose bottle doses itself as you fill it — no measuring needed.

Spray cleaning

  1. Fill a 750ml spray bottle with water.
  2. Turn the yellow head to the spray-bottle setting, pull it up fully, then press down to dose the concentrate into the bottle (≈1% solution).
  3. Shake gently before use.
  4. Remove any loose debris, spray onto the surface and clean with a microfibre cloth, sponge or brush.
  5. For worktop or floor areas, follow with a mop or dryer blade as usual.

Bucket cleaning

  1. Fill a bucket with 5L of warm water.
  2. Dose 2 doses (2×7.5ml) of concentrate (≈0.3% solution).
  3. Clean surfaces with a wetted cleaning cloth, or a mop/dryer blade for worktops and floors.

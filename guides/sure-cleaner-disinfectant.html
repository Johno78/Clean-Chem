SURE Cleaner Disinfectant is a concentrated, plant-based detergent-disinfectant that cleans and disinfects food premises surfaces in one step — worktops, chopping boards, equipment and utensils. It's effective in all water hardness and works well against bacteria, yeasts and viruses. Dosed through the Divermite (DvM) dispenser.

Before you start

  • The concentrate is acidic (pH ≈ 2) — wear gloves and eye protection when filling or handling the pouch.
  • Once diluted to the in-use strength, no special protective equipment is needed for normal use.
  • Give surfaces the full 5-minute contact time for the disinfectant to work properly.
  • For professional/trade use only.
  • Store the sealed pouch away from extremes of temperature.
  • Full safety information is in the product's Safety Data Sheet — ask us if you need a copy.

How to use it

Choose the method for the job — spray for everyday wipe-downs, or a bucket mix for larger surfaces.

Spray cleaning

  1. Fill a 750ml spray bottle with water and dose 1 dose (12ml) from the Divermite dispenser.
  2. Remove any loose debris first.
  3. Spray onto the surface and wipe with a damp cloth or brush.
  4. Leave for 5 minutes contact time.
  5. Rinse with clean water and leave to air dry.

Bucket cleaning

  1. Fill a bucket half full (3.5L) with hand-warm water and dose 3 doses (3×12ml) from the Divermite dispenser (≈1% solution).
  2. Remove any loose debris first.
  3. Apply the solution with a cloth or scrubbing brush.
  4. Leave for 5 minutes contact time.
  5. Rinse with clean water and leave to air dry.

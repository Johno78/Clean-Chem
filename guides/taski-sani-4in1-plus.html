TASKI Sani 4in1 Plus is a concentrated, acid-based washroom cleaner that cleans, descales, disinfects and deodorises all acid-resistant hard surfaces in one step. It uses Diversey's O.N.T. technology to neutralise bad odours rather than mask them. Dosed through the SmartDose bottle system.

Before you start

  • Acid-based (pH < 2) — wear gloves and eye protection when handling the concentrate.
  • Don't mix with other products.
  • For professional/trade use only.
  • Store away from extremes of temperature.
  • Full safety information is in the product's Safety Data Sheet — ask us if you need a copy.

How to use it

The SmartDose bottle doses itself as you fill it — no measuring needed.

Spray bottle

  1. Fill a 750ml spray bottle with water.
  2. Turn the yellow head to the spray-bottle setting, pull it up fully, then press down to dose the concentrate into the bottle.
  3. Shake gently before use.
  4. Spray onto the surface and wipe with a cloth, brush or sponge.
  5. Leave for 5 minutes contact time for disinfection.
  6. Rinse and leave to air dry.

Bucket

  1. Fill a bucket with water.
  2. Turn the yellow head to the bucket setting and press down to dose the concentrate in.
  3. Apply the solution with a cloth, sponge or mop.
  4. Brush briefly to distribute, and leave for 5 minutes contact time.
  5. Use a sponge pad on stubborn soiling.

Sanmex Fly & Wasp Killer is a fast-acting insecticide aerosol for wasps, midges and flies (including mosquitoes) in domestic and light commercial situations. Approved for use as directed under the Control of Pesticides Regulations 1986.

Before you start

  • Extremely flammable aerosol. Keep away from heat, sparks, open flames and hot surfaces — no smoking nearby.
  • Pressurised container — do not pierce or burn, even after use. Protect from sunlight; do not expose to temperatures over 50°C.
  • Do not spray on an open flame or other ignition source.
  • Very toxic to aquatic life — avoid contaminating water courses.
  • Contains Permethrin — may cause an allergic reaction in sensitive individuals.
  • Use only as an insecticide, as directed.

How to use it

Ready to use — no dilution needed.

Treating a room or insects directly

  1. Shake the can well before use.
  2. Hold the can upright and spray away from your body into the air, or directly at insects.
  3. A 3 second burst treats an average-sized room (30 cubic metres).
  4. Avoid direct contact with soft furnishings, carpets and polished surfaces.
  5. Do not use directly on clothing or bedding.

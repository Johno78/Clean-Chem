Suma Bac Conc D10 is a concentrated detergent-disinfectant that cleans and disinfects food premises surfaces in one step — worktops, food prep areas, fridge handles and similar. It's dosed through a Divermite dispenser, which mixes the concentrate to the right strength automatically so you don't need to measure it out by hand. This guide covers the two ways to use it.

Before you start

  • The neat concentrate is harmful if swallowed and can irritate skin and eyes — avoid direct contact with it, and wear gloves and eye protection when filling or handling the pouch.
  • Once diluted through the Divermite dispenser, no special protective equipment is needed for normal use.
  • For professional/trade use only.
  • Don't let the concentrate reach drains or watercourses undiluted — it's harmful to aquatic life.
  • Full safety information is in the product's Safety Data Sheet — ask us if you need a copy.

How to use it

Both methods use the Divermite dispenser to dose the concentrate — choose spray for everyday surfaces or soak for items you can submerge.

Spray cleaning & disinfection

  1. Fill the trigger spray bottle with water and insert it into the Divermite dispenser — push once to dispense the correct dose.
  2. Clear any loose debris from the surface first.
  3. Spray the solution onto the surface and wipe.
  4. Spray again and leave for a minimum of 30 seconds contact time.
  5. Wipe the surface a second time.
  6. Rinse with clean water and leave to air dry.
  7. A made-up spray bottle stays effective for up to 7 days — rinse and dry the bottle before refilling.

Soak cleaning & disinfection

  1. Fill a bucket or container with 3L of water and add two pushes of D10 from the Divermite dispenser using the product ladle.
  2. Clear any loose debris from the items first.
  3. Soak the items in the solution for a minimum of 5 minutes.
  4. Rinse with clean water and leave to air dry.

Adjust the dose and contact time for heavier soiling or tougher jobs where needed.

Domestos Professional Urinal Blocks are water-soluble channel blocks that counteract urine malodours and leave a fresh fragrance as they slowly dissolve. Simple to place, and won't clog outlets.

Before you start

  • Wear gloves and eye protection when handling.
  • For professional/trade use only.
  • Full safety information is in the product's Safety Data Sheet — ask us if you need a copy.

How to use it

No dilution or measuring — just place and go.

Individual urinals

  1. Use one block per urinal.
  2. Place it in the channel or over the drain outlet.

Troughs / channel urinals

  1. Place blocks approximately 50cm apart along the channel.

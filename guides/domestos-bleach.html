Domestos Professional Original Bleach

Neattoilets, drains, sinks

Domestos Professional Original Bleach is a thick gel bleach that clings to surfaces for longer contact time, with anti-limescale action. Use neat for disinfection on toilets, drains and sinks, or diluted for general surface cleaning, cloth/sponge soaking, and bleaching whites.

Before you start

  • Wear gloves and eye protection.
  • Never mix with other chemicals, especially anything acidic — this releases dangerous chlorine gas.
  • Do not use neat on floors.
  • Ensure good ventilation when using.
  • For professional/trade use only.
  • Full safety information is in the product's Safety Data Sheet — ask us if you need a copy.

How to use it

Use neat for disinfection in toilets and drains; dilute for general surface cleaning.

Toilets — neat

  1. Apply ≈80ml under the rim.
  2. Leave for 30 minutes to kill bacteria, fungi, viruses and spores.
  3. For best ongoing protection against limescale and germs, use at least once a day.

Drains & sinks — neat

  1. Drains: pour directly in to disinfect and eliminate odours.
  2. Sinks: pour into the plughole and overflow, leave 5 minutes, then rinse thoroughly.

Floors & work surfaces — diluted

  1. Dilute 180ml in 5L of water.
  2. Apply with a mop or cloth. Do not use neat on floors.

Dishcloths & sponges — diluted

  1. Dilute 90ml in half a bucket of water.
  2. Soak the cloth or sponge in the solution.

Bleaching whites — diluted

  1. Dilute 20ml in 5L of water.
  2. Soak overnight, then rinse thoroughly after use.

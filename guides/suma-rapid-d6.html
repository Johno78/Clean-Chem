Suma Rapid D6 is a ready-to-use, pH-neutral spray cleaner for glass, mirrors, ceramic, plastic and other water-resistant hard surfaces. It's excellent for removing fingerprints and light everyday marks, and dries fast without streaking. No dilution — just spray and wipe.

Before you start

  • Ready to use — no PPE needed for normal use.
  • Test on a small, inconspicuous area first if you're unsure about material compatibility.
  • For professional/trade use only.
  • Full safety information is in the product's Safety Data Sheet — ask us if you need a copy.

How to use it

Ready to use straight from the trigger bottle — no mixing needed.

Spray & wipe

  1. Spray directly onto the surface, or onto a soft cloth for delicate areas.
  2. Wipe clean.
  3. Repeat on stubborn marks if needed.

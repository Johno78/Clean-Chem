Room Care R2-plus Pur-Eco is a concentrated, weakly alkaline cleaner for all water-resistant hard surfaces — walls, doors, fittings and general surfaces. Non-abrasive and gentle enough for everyday use. It's dosed through the dedicated Room Care Divermite dispenser using the colour-coded R2 refill bottle.

Before you start

  • Mild in use — but still wear gloves when handling the concentrate.
  • Only use with the Room Care Divermite dispenser and the matching R2 (green) bottle — never with another dosing system.
  • For professional/trade use only.
  • Full safety information is in the product's Safety Data Sheet — ask us if you need a copy.

How to use it

The Room Care dispenser doses the concentrate straight into the colour-coded bottle — no measuring needed.

Spray cleaning

  1. Take the empty R2 (green) bottle, remove the spray head, and fill with cold water to the marked line (300ml).
  2. Place the bottle in the Room Care Divermite dispenser and press the button once to dose the concentrate (≈5% solution).
  3. Screw the spray head back on and shake gently to mix.
  4. Spray onto small areas and wipe with a cloth, brush or mop.
  5. Rinse food-contact surfaces with clean water and leave to air dry.

Bucket cleaning

  1. Fill a 5L bucket with water.
  2. Dose 1–2 doses (15–30ml) of concentrate into the bucket.
  3. Clean larger areas with a mop or cloth.
  4. Rinse and leave to air dry.

Chemical Toilet Flush is an additive for portable and chemical toilet waste tanks — used in event facilities, site cabins, and similar setups. It helps break down waste for easier emptying and controls odours between servicing.

Before you start

  • Wear gloves when handling.
  • Keep out of reach of children.
  • Full safety information is in the product's Safety Data Sheet — ask us if you need a copy.

How to use it

Dose into the waste tank, then top up with water to cover any solid matter.

Dosing the waste tank

  1. Add 100ml of fluid per 10L of waste tank capacity.
  2. Add water to fully cover any solid matter in the tank.
  3. One dose typically lasts several days, depending on usage — redose as needed.
  4. More or less solution may be needed depending on frequency of use.

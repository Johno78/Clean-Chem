Sun Professional Dishwasher Tablets are concentrated, pre-measured tablets for commercial dishwashers — effective in hard or soft water on stubborn stains like coffee, tea and dried-on food. For the best results, pair with Sun Professional Rinse Aid for a streak-free shine and Sun Professional Salt to prevent limescale.

Before you start

  • Keep tablets in their sachets until use, out of reach of children.
  • For professional/trade use only.
  • Full safety information is in the product's Safety Data Sheet — ask us if you need a copy.

How to use it

No measuring — one tablet per cycle.

Dishwasher cycle

  1. Remove one tablet from its sachet just before use. Dispose of the sachet.
  2. Place the tablet in the dishwasher's detergent compartment.
  3. Close the compartment and run the cycle as normal.
  4. For best results, keep the rinse aid and salt compartments topped up.

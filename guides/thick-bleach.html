Thick Bleach is a thickened-formula bleach that clings to vertical surfaces for effective contact time. Use it neat for disinfecting toilets, drains and pipes, or diluted for general surface cleaning and whitening stained fabric.

Before you start

  • Wear gloves and eye protection.
  • Never mix with other chemicals, especially anything acidic — this releases dangerous chlorine gas.
  • Ensure good ventilation when using.
  • Full safety information is in the product's Safety Data Sheet — ask us if you need a copy.

How to use it

Use neat for disinfection, or dilute for general cleaning.

Toilets, drains & pipes — neat

  1. Apply directly, undiluted.
  2. Leave to work, then flush or rinse.

General surfaces & floors — diluted

  1. Dilute 1 part bleach to 20 parts water.
  2. Apply with a mop or cloth.
  3. Rinse if needed on surfaces sensitive to bleach.

Comfort Prof Concentrated Blue Skies is a fabric softener built for autodose machines — the wall-mounted or floor-standing dosing pump handles the measuring, so there's nothing for staff to dose by hand. Your job is just keeping the canister connected and changed over when empty.

Before you start

  • Don't pour or dose this manually — it's designed for the connected autodose pump system.
  • Wear gloves when changing the canister or handling the concentrate.
  • For professional/trade use only.
  • Full safety information is in the product's Safety Data Sheet — ask us if you need a copy.

How to use it

The autodose pump measures and injects the correct amount automatically during the wash cycle.

Changing the canister

  1. When the low-level alarm sounds (or the canister runs dry), disconnect the suction tube from the empty canister.
  2. Fit the tube to the new 10L canister, making sure the connection is secure.
  3. Prime the system following your dosing unit's instructions if needed.
  4. Check the machine completes a normal wash and rinse cycle without error.

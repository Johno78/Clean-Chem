Vanish Gold Crystal Powder is an Oxi Action stain remover and laundry booster, used alongside your normal detergent rather than instead of it. We stock both the Colours and White formulas — Colours is safe for coloured fabrics, White is formulated to keep whites looking brighter. Both use the same methods.

Before you start

  • Do not swallow.
  • Clean and dry the scoop before placing it back in the tub.
  • Keep out of reach of children.

How to use it

Three methods depending on how tough the stain is.

In-wash (routine use)

  1. Add 1 scoop (≈60g) to the drum or dispenser drawer alongside your normal detergent.
  2. Run the wash as normal.

Soak (tough or set-in stains)

  1. Dissolve 1 scoop in 7L of water.
  2. Soak whites or colours for around 2 hours.
  3. Rub the stain before washing for best results, then wash as normal.

Pre-treat (direct on the stain)

  1. Mix ½ scoop (≈30g) with 1½ scoops of water and stir for 30 seconds.
  2. Apply to the stain for a maximum of 5 minutes, rubbing as needed.
  3. Wash as normal afterwards.

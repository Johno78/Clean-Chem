Room Care R1-plus Pur-Eco is a concentrated, citric acid-based toilet and urinal cleaner for daily use. It's dosed through the dedicated Room Care Divermite dispenser, using the colour-coded R1 refill bottle so it can't be mixed up with the other Room Care products. Regular use helps prevent limescale build-up.

Before you start

  • Acidic in use (pH ≈ 2) — wear gloves and eye protection when handling the concentrate.
  • Not suitable for acid-sensitive surfaces — enamel, marble and grout. Wipe off any splashes on these surfaces immediately and rinse with water.
  • Don't mix with bleach or other cleaning products.
  • Only use with the Room Care Divermite dispenser and the matching R1 (red) bottle — never with another dosing system.
  • For professional/trade use only.
  • Full safety information is in the product's Safety Data Sheet — ask us if you need a copy.

How to use it

The Room Care dispenser doses the concentrate straight into the colour-coded bottle — no measuring needed.

Daily cleaning

  1. Take the empty R1 (red) bottle, remove the spray head, and fill with cold water to the marked line (750ml).
  2. Place the bottle in the Room Care Divermite dispenser and press the button once to dose the concentrate (≈2% solution).
  3. Screw the spray head back on and shake gently to mix.
  4. Apply to the toilet or urinal and clean with a brush or cloth.
  5. Flush or rinse away.

Tougher dirt & limescale

  1. Fill the R1 bottle with cold water only to the 300ml line instead.
  2. Dose as above (≈5% solution) for extra strength.
  3. Apply, leave briefly to work, then scrub and rinse away.

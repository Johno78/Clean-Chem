Fairy Platinum Dishwasher Tablets are all-in-one tablets combining detergent, rinse aid and salt action in one dose — no need to separately top up rinse aid or dishwasher salt for most water hardness levels. The outer film dissolves during the wash, so there's no unwrapping.

Before you start

  • Keep tablets in their original packaging until use, out of reach of children.
  • Do not cut or break tablets.

How to use it

No measuring — just one tablet per cycle.

Running a cycle

  1. Place one tablet in the dishwasher's detergent compartment.
  2. Close the compartment and start the cycle as normal — the film dissolves during the wash.
  3. In very hard water areas, you may still want to top up dishwasher salt separately for best results.

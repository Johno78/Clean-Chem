TASKI Tapi Gum Remover is a cooling aerosol spray that freezes chewing gum solid so it can be chipped away cleanly — no scrubbing, no residue, no damage to carpet fibres. CFC-free and WoolSafe approved, so it's safe on wool carpets too.

Before you start

  • Extremely flammable aerosol. Keep away from heat, sparks, open flames and hot surfaces — no smoking nearby.
  • Do not spray near an open flame or other ignition source.
  • Pressurised container — do not pierce or burn, even after use.
  • For professional/trade use only.
  • Full safety information is in the product's Safety Data Sheet — ask us if you need a copy.

How to use it

Freeze it solid, then chip it away — the gum should lift cleanly without pulling on the carpet fibres.

Removing gum from carpet

  1. Remove as much gum as possible first with a scraper or dull knife.
  2. Shake the can before and during use.
  3. Insert the extension tube into the valve tip.
  4. Point the nozzle at a 45° angle, within 2 inches of the gum.
  5. Spray in short, quick bursts until the gum is frozen solid.
  6. Chip or scrape off the frozen gum with a putty knife or dull scraper.

Pine Gel Floor Cleaner is a neutral-pH floor cleaner for regular maintenance of vinyl, rubber, sealed cork, sealed wood and thermoplastic floors. It cleans and deodorises in one step, removing dirt and heel marks, and buffs up to a slip-retardant shine.

Before you start

  • Not suitable for unsealed wood or cork.
  • Wear gloves when handling the concentrate.
  • Full safety information is in the product's Safety Data Sheet — ask us if you need a copy.

How to use it

Dilute before mopping — strength depends on the method.

Damp mopping

  1. Dilute 100ml per 5L of warm water.
  2. Mop the floor.
  3. When dry, burnish to a shine if required.

Spray cleaning

  1. Dilute 10ml per 500ml of cold water in a spray bottle.
  2. Spray the floor lightly.
  3. Buff to a shine.

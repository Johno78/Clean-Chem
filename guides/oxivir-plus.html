Oxivir Plus is a broad-spectrum, one-step cleaner and disinfectant based on Diversey's Accelerated Hydrogen Peroxide (AHP) technology. It's effective against bacteria, viruses (including coronavirus and norovirus) and fungi, and is safe on all acid-resistant surfaces including upholstery and carpets. Ideal for outbreak prevention and body spillage clean-up. It's dosed through the SmartDose bottle system.

Before you start

  • The concentrate is acidic — wear gloves and eye protection when handling it or fitting a new SmartDose bottle.
  • Not suitable for marble or aluminium — these are acid-sensitive materials.
  • No rinsing needed — the active ingredient breaks down to water and oxygen after use.
  • Fragrance-free and dye-free.
  • For professional/trade use only.
  • Store away from extremes of temperature.
  • Full safety information is in the product's Safety Data Sheet — ask us if you need a copy.

How to use it

The SmartDose bottle doses itself as you fill it — no measuring needed.

Cleaning & disinfecting a surface

  1. Fill a 750ml spray bottle with water.
  2. Turn the yellow head to the spray-bottle setting, pull it up fully, then press down to dose the concentrate into the bottle.
  3. Shake gently before use.
  4. Pre-clean heavily soiled areas first.
  5. Spray onto the surface, making sure it's thoroughly wet, and wipe with a cloth.
  6. Leave the surface wet for 5 minutes contact time.
  7. Wipe dry or allow to air dry — no rinsing needed.

Change the solution once it looks visibly dirty or diluted.

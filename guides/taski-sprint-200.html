TASKI Sprint 200 is a highly concentrated, alcohol-based cleaner for daily streak-free cleaning of glass and all water-resistant hard surfaces and floors. It's part of Diversey's EXACT dosing system — a metered cap that screws onto the container and dispenses one measured "cap" of concentrate at a time.

Before you start

  • Wear gloves when handling the concentrate.
  • For professional/trade use only.
  • Store away from extremes of temperature.
  • Full safety information is in the product's Safety Data Sheet — ask us if you need a copy.

How to use it

The EXACT cap doses a measured amount with each squeeze — no measuring cups needed.

Spray bottle

  1. Fill a 500ml spray bottle with water.
  2. Add 1 cap (2.0%) of concentrate using the EXACT dosing cap.
  3. Spray onto the surface and wipe with a cloth or mop.
  4. Leave to air dry — no rinsing needed.

Bucket or floor machine

  1. Fill a bucket or machine tank with 10L of water.
  2. Add 2 caps (0.2%) of concentrate.
  3. Apply with a mop or floor machine.
  4. Leave to air dry.

TASKI Sprint 200 is a highly concentrated, alcohol-based cleaner for daily streak-free cleaning of glass and all water-resistant hard surfaces and floors. Dosed through the SmartDose bottle system — one 1.4L bottle makes roughly 280 spray bottles or 93 sinks/buckets.

Before you start

  • Wear gloves when handling the concentrate.
  • For professional/trade use only.
  • Store away from extremes of temperature.
  • Full safety information is in the product's Safety Data Sheet — ask us if you need a copy.

How to use it

The SmartDose bottle doses itself as you fill it — no measuring needed.

Spray bottle

  1. Fill a spray bottle with water.
  2. Turn the yellow head to the spray-bottle setting, pull it up fully, then press down to dose the concentrate into the bottle (≈1:100 dilution).
  3. Shake gently before use.
  4. Spray onto the surface and wipe with a cloth or mop.
  5. Leave to air dry — no rinsing needed.

Bucket or sink

  1. Fill a bucket or sink with water.
  2. Turn the yellow head to the bucket setting and press down to dose the concentrate in (≈1:333 dilution).
  3. Apply with a mop or floor machine.
  4. Leave to air dry.

Milton Disinfecting Fluid is a sodium hypochlorite-based disinfectant for hospitals and professional kitchens — work surfaces, floors, walls and utensils. It kills bacteria, fungi and viruses. No heating and no rinsing needed once items come out of solution.

Before you start

  • Do not use on metal — it can cause corrosion.
  • Never mix with other products — this can release toxic chlorine gas.
  • Avoid contact with eyes and skin; splashes may discolour fabric.
  • Use a non-metallic container for the solution.
  • Full safety information is in the product's Safety Data Sheet — ask us if you need a copy.

How to use it

Make up a fresh solution and renew it every 24 hours.

Sterilising equipment & utensils

  1. Wash items thoroughly in warm, soapy water first, then rinse with cold water.
  2. Fill a non-metallic container with 5L of cold water and add 30ml (one capful) of Milton fluid.
  3. Fully submerge the items, making sure there are no trapped air bubbles.
  4. Leave for 15 minutes. No need to rinse — items are ready to use straight away.
  5. Items can stay in the solution until needed. Renew the solution every 24 hours.

Disinfecting surfaces

  1. Fill a container with 5L of cold water and add 90ml (three capfuls) of Milton fluid.
  2. Make sure the surface is clean first, then apply with a cloth, fully wetting the surface.
  3. Leave for 5–15 minutes, then rinse — no need to rinse surfaces in direct food contact.
  4. The solution stays effective for 24 hours.

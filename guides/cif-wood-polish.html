Cif Prof. Wood Furniture Polish cleans, polishes and protects wood furniture and surfaces, leaving a streak-free shine. We stock it in two ready-to-use formats — a 400ml aerosol and a 750ml trigger spray — both used the same way.

Before you start

  • For professional/trade use only.
  • Full safety information is in the product's Safety Data Sheet — ask us if you need a copy.

How to use it

Ready to use straight from the can or bottle — no mixing needed.

Polishing wood surfaces

  1. Shake well before use.
  2. Spray a light, even coat onto the wood surface, or apply to a cloth first for detailed areas.
  3. Wipe with a clean, dry cloth to buff to a shine.

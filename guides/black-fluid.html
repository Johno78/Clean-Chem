Black Fluid cleans driveways, paths and patios, and neutralises odours from stains — leaving them hygienically clean. Outdoor use only — do not use indoors.

Before you start

  • Outdoor use only — do not use indoors.
  • Wear gloves and eye protection.
  • Keep away from pets, especially cats and fish, and away from plants — it can be harmful to aquatic life and plant life.
  • Protect from frost; store in a cool, dry place.
  • Full safety information is in the product's Safety Data Sheet — ask us if you need a copy.

How to use it

One dilution rate covers driveways, paths and general outdoor stains.

Driveways, paths & outdoor stains

  1. Dilute 60ml Black Fluid to 5L water.
  2. Mop onto the surface.
  3. Always rinse off thoroughly with water.

Toilet Duck 5in1 Rim Block attaches under the toilet rim and releases a foaming, fragranced clean with every flush — no daily dosing needed. Marine fragrance, lasting several weeks before it needs replacing.

Before you start

  • For use only in toilet bowls.
  • Wash and dry hands after handling.
  • Keep out of reach of children — the block may be attractive to young children.

How to use it

One-time fitting — then it works with every flush until it's used up.

Fitting the block

  1. Unfold the hook on the back of the block.
  2. Attach it to the inside edge of the toilet bowl, positioned in the flow of water from the cistern.
  3. Flush to activate — a fresh, foaming clean is released with every flush.
  4. Replace with a new block once it has fully dissolved.

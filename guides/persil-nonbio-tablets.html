Persil Prof Non Bio Tablets

Laundry tablets — 3x56 (168 tablets)
2 tabletsper normal load

Non-biosensitive skin

Persil Prof Non Bio Tablets are pre-measured laundry tablets — no scooping or pouring, just drop them in. The non-biological formula (no enzymes) is dermatologically tested and suited to sensitive skin, with brightening agents to keep whites looking sharp.

Before you start

  • Keep tablets in their original packaging until use, out of reach of children.
  • Not suitable for silk or wool.
  • For professional/trade use only.
  • Full safety information is in the product's Safety Data Sheet — ask us if you need a copy.

How to use it

No measuring — just the right number of tablets for the load.

Machine wash

  1. Place 2 tablets directly into the drum (or the dispenser drawer if your machine's manual recommends it) for a normal load.
  2. Load the washing according to the garment care labels.
  3. Choose the appropriate wash cycle and temperature.
  4. Run the wash as normal.

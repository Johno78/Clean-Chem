Sulphuric Acid Drain Cleaner is a highly concentrated acid formulation (typically 90%+ sulphuric acid) for rapidly clearing stubborn blockages in outdoor drains, pipes and toilets. It's a serious hazard if handled carelessly — read this guide fully before use, and only use it if you're trained and equipped to do so safely.

Before you start — this one matters

  • Highly corrosive. Causes severe skin burns and serious eye damage. Wear chemical-resistant gloves, eye/face protection and protective clothing.
  • Trade and professional use only — this product is regulated and restricted to trade users.
  • Outdoor drains, pipes and toilets only. Not suitable for septic tanks.
  • Only use with plastic plumbing — it can damage chrome, stainless steel and galvanised pipework.
  • If diluting, always add the acid to water, never water to acid — reversing this can cause violent spitting and splashing.
  • Clear the area around the drain and cover any nearby metal fittings before use.
  • Never mix with other chemicals, especially bleach — this can release toxic gas.
  • Work in a well-ventilated area.
  • Full safety information is in the product's Safety Data Sheet — ask us if you need a copy.

How to use it

Used neat, in small amounts, poured slowly and carefully.

Clearing a blocked outdoor drain, pipe or toilet

  1. Put on full PPE — chemical-resistant gloves, eye/face protection and protective clothing.
  2. Ensure the area is well ventilated and clear of people and pets.
  3. Pour a small amount of the product slowly and directly into the blocked drain, pipe or toilet — do not rush this step.
  4. Leave for 10–15 minutes to dissolve the blockage.
  5. Flush thoroughly with plenty of cold water.
  6. Repeat only if necessary, and never exceed the amount needed to clear the blockage.

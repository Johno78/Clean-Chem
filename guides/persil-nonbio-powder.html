Persil Non Bio Washing Powder is a dermatologically tested laundry powder, gentle on sensitive skin while still tackling tough stains in cold washes. Dosed into the machine's dispenser drawer — the amount depends on soil level and water hardness.

Before you start

  • Avoid contact with eyes — rinse thoroughly with water if it happens.
  • Wear gloves for hand washing.
  • Not suitable for silk or wool.
  • For professional/trade use only.
  • Full safety information is in the product's Safety Data Sheet — ask us if you need a copy.

How to use it

Use the box's dosing table for exact amounts — as a starting point:

Machine wash

  1. Check garment care labels and choose the appropriate wash cycle and temperature.
  2. Add 50g of powder to the dispenser drawer for a normal load, medium soiling and medium-hard water.
  3. Increase the dose for heavier soiling or harder water — check the pack's dosing table.
  4. Avoid underfilling the machine, and wash at lower temperatures where possible.

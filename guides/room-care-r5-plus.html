Room Care R5-plus

Non-aerosol air freshener — concentrate

Fabric-safecolourfast, washable fabrics

Room Care R5-plus is a concentrated, non-aerosol spray air freshener. It uses O.N.T. technology to neutralise bad odours rather than just masking them, leaving a light, fresh fragrance behind. It's safe on colourfast washable fabrics and furniture. It's dosed through the dedicated Room Care Divermite dispenser using the colour-coded R5 refill bottle.

Before you start

  • For use on colourfast, washable fabrics and furniture only — spot-test anything you're unsure about first.
  • Only use with the Room Care Divermite dispenser and the matching R5 bottle — never with another dosing system.
  • For professional/trade use only.
  • Full safety information is in the product's Safety Data Sheet — ask us if you need a copy.

How to use it

The Room Care dispenser doses the concentrate straight into the colour-coded bottle — no measuring needed.

Refreshing a room

  1. Take the empty R5 bottle, remove the spray head, and fill with water to the marked line (300ml).
  2. Place the bottle in the Room Care Divermite dispenser and press the button once to dose the concentrate (≈5–10% solution).
  3. Screw the spray head back on and shake gently to mix.
  4. Spray lightly into the air, or onto fabrics and furnishings, to neutralise odours and freshen the space.

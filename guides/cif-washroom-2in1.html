How to use it

Ready to use straight from the trigger bottle — no mixing needed.

Cleaning & descaling

  1. Spray directly onto the surface — tiles, sinks, taps, shower screens.
  2. Wipe with a cloth or sponge, agitating limescale spots if needed.
  3. Rinse if needed on chrome or stainless steel fittings.

TASKI Sani MouldOut is a ready-to-use, chlorine-based grout cleaner that removes mould, mildew and fungus from tiles, tile joints and shower cubicles — also suitable for cleaning shower curtains. Its high-foaming formula clings to vertical surfaces for better contact time. No dilution needed — just spray, wait and rinse.

Before you start

  • Wear gloves and eye protection — this is a chlorine-based product.
  • Don't mix with acidic cleaners.
  • Only use on dry surfaces, in a well-ventilated area — avoid inhaling the spray.
  • For professional/trade use only.
  • Full safety information is in the product's Safety Data Sheet — ask us if you need a copy.

How to use it

Ready to use straight from the trigger bottle — no mixing needed.

Removing mould & mildew

  1. Remove any loose soiling first.
  2. Spray directly onto tiles, grout lines, cubicles or shower curtains.
  3. Leave for a few minutes to work.
  4. Rinse thoroughly with clean water.
  5. Wipe with a clean cloth.

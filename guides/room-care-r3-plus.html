Room Care R3-plus Pur-Eco is a concentrated, near-neutral glass and multi-surface cleaner. It's very effective at removing fingerprints and greasy marks, dries fast and leaves no streaks. It's dosed through the dedicated Room Care Divermite dispenser using the colour-coded R3 refill bottle.

Before you start

  • Near-neutral pH — mild in use, but still wear gloves when handling the concentrate.
  • Not for use on water-sensitive surfaces such as untreated wood.
  • Only use with the Room Care Divermite dispenser and the matching R3 (blue) bottle — never with another dosing system.
  • For professional/trade use only.
  • Full safety information is in the product's Safety Data Sheet — ask us if you need a copy.

How to use it

The Room Care dispenser doses the concentrate straight into the colour-coded bottle — no measuring needed.

Spray & wipe

  1. Take the empty R3 (blue) bottle, remove the spray head, and fill with cold water to the marked line (750ml).
  2. Place the bottle in the Room Care Divermite dispenser and press the button once to dose the concentrate (≈2% solution).
  3. Screw the spray head back on and shake gently to mix.
  4. Spray a small amount onto the surface and wipe clean with a cloth.
  5. No rinsing needed — it dries fast without streaks.

SURE Interior & Surface Cleaner is a concentrated, plant-based all-purpose detergent for cleaning hard surfaces — floors, walls, doors, ceilings and general equipment. Unlike SURE Cleaner & Degreaser, it's safe on aluminium and other soft metals, so it's the one to reach for on those surfaces. We stock it in two formats — as a Divermite pouch, and as a SmartDose bottle — so check which dispenser you're using below.

Before you start

  • Mild in use — but still wear gloves and eye protection when handling the concentrate.
  • Safe for use on aluminium and other soft metals (brass, copper).
  • For professional/trade use only.
  • Store away from extremes of temperature.
  • Full safety information is in the product's Safety Data Sheet — ask us if you need a copy.

How to use it

Two dispensing formats, same cleaner — use whichever matches the equipment on your wall.

Divermite pouch (2L)

  1. Spray bottle: fill 750ml with water and dose from the Divermite dispenser to give a 1% solution.
  2. Bucket: dose to give a 0.3–1.5% solution depending on how soiled the area is.
  3. Remove loose debris, apply the solution, and clean with a cloth, brush, mop or floor machine.
  4. Rinse food-contact surfaces with clean water and leave to air dry.

SmartDose bottle (1.4L)

  1. Fill a 750ml spray bottle with water.
  2. Turn the yellow head to the spray-bottle setting, pull it up fully, then press down to dose the concentrate into the bottle.
  3. Shake the bottle gently before use.
  4. Remove loose debris, spray onto the surface and wipe with a cloth.
  5. Rinse food-contact surfaces with clean water and leave to air dry.

TASKI Sprint Flower SD is a pH-neutral, highly perfumed cleaning and deodorising concentrate for a wide range of hard surfaces and glass — natural and artificial stone, ceramic tile, synthetic surfaces, mirrors, porcelain and enamel. Dries quickly without streaking and leaves a strong, fresh fragrance. Dosed through the SmartDose bottle system — one 1.4L bottle makes roughly 280 spray bottles or 100 buckets.

Before you start

  • Wear gloves and eye protection when handling the concentrate or fitting a new SmartDose bottle.
  • For professional/trade use only.
  • Store away from extremes of temperature.
  • Full safety information is in the product's Safety Data Sheet — ask us if you need a copy.

How to use it

The SmartDose bottle doses itself as you fill it — no measuring needed.

Spray cleaning

  1. Fill a 750ml spray bottle with water.
  2. Turn the yellow head to the spray-bottle setting, pull it up fully, then press down to dose the concentrate into the bottle.
  3. Shake gently before use.
  4. Spray onto the surface and wipe clean with a cloth.

Bucket cleaning

  1. Fill a bucket with water.
  2. Turn the yellow head to the bucket setting and press down to dose the concentrate in.
  3. Apply with a mop or cloth.

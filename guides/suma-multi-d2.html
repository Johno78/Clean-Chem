Suma Multi Conc D2 is a highly concentrated all-purpose cleaner for hard surfaces in food premises — floors, walls, doors, ceilings and general kitchen equipment. It cuts through grease and dried-on food and works in all water conditions. It's dosed through a Divermite or Diverflow dispenser, so you don't need to measure it out by hand.

Before you start

  • The neat concentrate is alkaline — avoid direct contact with it, and wear gloves and eye protection when filling or handling the pouch.
  • Once diluted through the dispenser, no special protective equipment is needed for normal use.
  • For professional/trade use only.
  • Not suitable for polished aluminium.
  • Store the sealed pouch away from extremes of temperature.
  • Full safety information is in the product's Safety Data Sheet — ask us if you need a copy.

How to use it

Choose the method for the job — spray for everyday wipe-downs, or a bucket mix for tables, floors and larger equipment.

Spray cleaning

  1. From the Divermite dispenser: fill a spray bottle with 1 dose of 5ml in 750ml of water.
  2. Spray the solution onto the surface and wipe clean with a damp cloth.
  3. Rinse food-contact surfaces well with clean water and leave to air dry.

General & floor cleaning

  1. From the Divermite dispenser: fill a bucket with 2 doses in 5L of water — adjust the dose upward for heavily soiled areas.
  2. Apply the solution with a spray bottle, cloth, brush or mop.
  3. Scrub to remove stubborn deposits.
  4. Rinse with clean water and leave to air dry.

Suma Silver D8 is a detarnishing dip for silver and silver-plated items — cutlery, serving dishes, table decorations and similar. A blend of acid, reducing agent and surfactant lifts tarnish, black spots and pitting without scratching or the need for polishing. Used neat, straight from the container.

Before you start

  • Used neat — wear gloves and eye protection when handling it.
  • Only use in a plastic container — the acid content can attack metal containers.
  • Clear up any spillages promptly to prevent staining.
  • For professional/trade use only.
  • Full safety information is in the product's Safety Data Sheet — ask us if you need a copy.

How to use it

No dilution needed — just a plastic container and a quick dip.

Detarnishing silverware

  1. Fill a plastic container with Suma Silver D8.
  2. Immerse the tarnished items in the neat product for 30–60 seconds.
  3. Rinse thoroughly with clean water and dry, or wash in a dishwasher using a detergent without bleach.
  4. Replace the solution once it's exhausted.

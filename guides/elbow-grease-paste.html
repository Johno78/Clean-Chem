Elbow Grease Cleaning Paste is a versatile degreasing paste for tough jobs — sinks, cooker tops, baking trays, barbecues, ovens, tiles and outdoor furniture. It also lifts rust and limescale from metal surfaces. A little goes a long way.

Before you start

  • Mildly abrasive — test on an inconspicuous area first, especially on delicate or painted surfaces.
  • Wear gloves for prolonged contact.

How to use it

Ready to use — apply a small amount and work it in.

General cleaning & degreasing

  1. Apply a small amount of paste to a damp cloth or sponge.
  2. Rub onto the surface, using extra pressure on stubborn areas.
  3. Wipe clean with a damp cloth, then dry.

Flash All Purpose Liquid is a concentrated cleaner that cuts through grease and general soiling on hard surfaces, leaving a fresh scent and a streak-free shine. Flexible to use — neat for tough jobs, or diluted right down for everyday cleaning.

Before you start

  • Wear gloves for prolonged contact.
  • Full safety information is in the product's Safety Data Sheet — ask us if you need a copy.

How to use it

Works in hot or cold water — dilute according to the job.

Tough grease & stubborn soiling

  1. Apply neat to the affected area.
  2. Leave briefly to work, then wipe or rinse clean.

General cleaning

  1. Dilute up to 1 part liquid to 100 parts water, depending on soiling.
  2. Apply with a mop or cloth.
  3. Rinse or wipe as needed.
